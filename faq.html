Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about searching for and booking a sublet.

How do I find a sublet?

Visit the Find a Sublet page and browse the available accommodation options.

Are the sublets verified?

SubletMate presents verified and up-to-date sublet options to help users find a reliable place to stay.

Which destinations are available?

The website currently includes sublets in Tel Aviv, Jerusalem, and Haifa.

How do I ask about a specific sublet?

Select the relevant accommodation and use the Contact Us form to send your details.

Can I request a short or long stay?

Yes. In the contact form, you can indicate whether you need the sublet for up to one week or for more than one week.
